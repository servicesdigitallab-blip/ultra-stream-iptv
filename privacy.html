Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 2026 • Official Ultra Stream Policy

1 Commitment to User Privacy

At Ultra Stream, we value your privacy above all else. This Privacy Policy details how we collect, handle, and protect your information when you use our website and IPTV streaming services.

Strict Zero-Logs Policy: We do not log, monitor, or track the TV channels, movies, sports, or content you watch. Your streaming activity remains 100% private and confidential.

2 Information We Collect

To provide our services and complete our 10-Minute Fast Delivery Guarantee, we only collect the minimum required information:

  • Email Address: To deliver your M3U playlist URL, Xtream Codes API credentials, and portal details within 10 minutes.

3 10-Minute Fast Delivery Policy

Upon receipt of payment, our automated server infrastructure generates and delivers your login credentials to your specified email within 10 minutes. Transactional emails are strictly used for credential delivery, setup guides, and renewal reminders.

4 Data Protection & Security

All data exchanged on our website is secured with enterprise-grade 256-bit SSL (TLS 1.3) encryption. We do not sell, rent, trade, or share your contact details with any third parties or advertisers.

5 Contacting Support

If you have any questions or wish to request data removal, our VIP Support team is available 24/7 via live support and email.
